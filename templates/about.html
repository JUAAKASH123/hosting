The idea

What is VirtuFit?

VirtuFit brings the concept of a virtual fitting room into a simple, camera-based web experience.

Instead of physically changing clothes every time, users can select different garments and see them overlaid on their live camera view.

Computer vision helps track the user's body position while the interface makes switching between outfits quick and interactive.

Inside the system

How the experience comes together.

A simple pipeline connects the camera, body tracking and virtual garment.

01 — CAMERA

Capture

The browser receives the live camera feed after the user grants permission.

02 — TRACK

Detect Pose

MediaPipe detects relevant body landmarks from the camera frames.

03 — ALIGN

Position Garment

The selected garment is scaled and positioned using the detected body information.

04 — INTERACT

Change Outfit

Users can switch garments with gestures or keyboard controls.
