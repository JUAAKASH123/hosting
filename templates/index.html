Before you start

A few quick things for the best experience:

  • 📷Your camera runs entirely on this device — nothing is uploaded or stored anywhere.
  • 🧍Stand back far enough that your shoulders and hips are both visible in frame.
  • 💡Good, even lighting helps the AI track you accurately — avoid strong backlight.
  • 👉Point at Prev/Next to switch outfits hands-free, or just tap/click them.
  • ⌨️On desktop, the ← and → arrow keys also switch outfits.
